AI or Human?

In this "AI or Human" experiment, brought to you by Northwestern University's Department of Computer Science, you will be presented with a series of short social-media texts. Each text was written either by a person or generated by AI. Our aim is to understand how well people can distinguish human-written text from AI-written text, and to evaluate how AI-generated explanations impact this detection process. We ask you to read each text carefully and share whether you believe it was written by a Human or by an AI. During the experiment, you may also be provided with an AI-generated explanation hint to assist your analysis.

Informed Consent

This research is conducted by Northwestern University's Department of Computer Science. All data is collected anonymously for research purposes. Participation is voluntary, and you may only participate if you are 18 years of age or older. For questions, see more on Informed Consent or please contact Professor Jessica Hullman at jessica.hullman@northwestern.edu.
