AI or Human?

How can you tell if a short social-media text was written by a person or generated by AI? How well can people distinguish human writing from AI writing based on tone, word choice, and style? This is a research project hosted at Northwestern University by researchers in the Computer Science Department.

Research Team

Dong Shu, Ziyang Guo, Negar Kamali, Matt Groh, Jessica Hullman
